Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 June 2026

Soma PDF is a local-first desktop application. We designed it so that your documents and personal information stay on your own computer.

What we collect

Nothing. Soma PDF does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal information or document content. There are no user accounts, no sign-in, and no analytics or telemetry.

How your documents are handled

Every operation — opening, reading, editing, annotating, converting, OCR, signing, redaction, and protection — happens entirely on your device. Your PDFs and any files you add are never uploaded to us or to any third party. Files are read from and written to the locations you choose on your own machine.

Licensing

If you activate the app with a license key, the key is validated offline on your device. No license or activation data is sent to any server.

Network access

Soma PDF works fully offline. The only optional outbound connection is an application-update check, which may contact the update service to ask whether a newer version exists. This check sends no document content and no personal information. When the app is installed from the Microsoft Store, updates are delivered by the Store and this check is not used.

The in-app “View online” link for the Terms of Use opens your default web browser to a public web page only when you click it.

Children’s privacy

Soma PDF is a general-purpose productivity tool and is not directed at children. It collects no information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this same URL with a new “Last updated” date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed to the support contact listed on the Soma PDF Microsoft Store page.
