Soma PDF

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 June 2026

Soma PDF is a local-first desktop application. We designed it so that your documents and personal information stay on your own computer.

What we collect

Nothing. Soma PDF does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal information or document content. There are no user accounts, no sign-in, and no analytics or telemetry.

How your documents are handled

Every operation — opening, reading, editing, annotating, converting, OCR, translation, signing, redaction, protection, and the built-in AI assistant — happens entirely on your device by default. Your PDFs and any files you add are never uploaded to us, and OCR, translation, and the built-in AI assistant run locally on your own machine. The one exception is the optional cloud AI tier described under “Network access” below — it is off by default, and your document text is sent out only for a document you explicitly turn it on for. Files are read from and written to the locations you choose on your own machine.

Licensing

If you activate the app with a license key, the key is validated offline on your device. No license or activation data is sent to any server.

Network access

Soma PDF works fully offline by default. The model download and update check below send no document content or personal information; the optional cloud AI tier sends document text only for documents you explicitly opt in:

AI model download. The first time you open the on-device AI assistant, the app downloads its language model once from a public host and stores it locally. This is a one-time download of the model file only; after it completes, the assistant runs fully offline. The OCR and translation models are bundled with the app and require no download.

Application-update check. The app may contact the update service to ask whether a newer version exists. When the app is installed from the Microsoft Store, updates are delivered by the Store and this check is not used.

Optional cloud AI (off by default). If you choose to add your own cloud provider API key (Settings → AI) and then turn on the cloud tier for a specific document, that document’s text and your question are sent to the provider you chose (e.g. Groq or Cerebras) to generate a response. This happens only for documents you explicitly opt in — one at a time, after a confirmation that warns you the text leaves your device — and is intended for non-confidential documents only. Your API key is stored encrypted on your device and is never sent to us. Soma PDF operates no servers of its own and never receives this data; it is handled under your chosen provider’s privacy policy.

The in-app “View online” link for the Terms of Use opens your default web browser to a public web page only when you click it.

Children’s privacy

Soma PDF is a general-purpose productivity tool and is not directed at children. It collects no information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this same URL with a new “Last updated” date.

Contact
